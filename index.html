Questions

Can I write in Hebrew or Yiddish?

Yes. Every simcha comes with full ready-made wording in Lashon Kodesh, Yiddish and English, and you can change any word. Right-to-left text lines up on its own, and the Hebrew date fills in from the English date.

How do I send it on WhatsApp?

Tap Download JPEG. On a phone, choose “Save Image” and the card goes into your photos. Then send it on WhatsApp like any picture.

Can I print it?

Yes. Tap Download PDF and print it at home or send it to a printer. The JPEG prints well too.

Can I add a photo or logo?

Yes. Add it in step 5 and it shows on both the JPEG and the PDF.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, with no account and no app.

Is my information saved anywhere?

No. The card is made right on your phone or computer. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
